# ADR-007 — Regional deployment cell as the failure and residency domain

## Context

The platform needs a bounded failure domain and a bounded residency domain. Blueprint 03 sets regional cells with active/standby execution ownership; NFR-PRV-01 and NFR-GLO-01 require residency per tenant region; D-056 places the bitemporal market-data store and its archive inside the tenant's regional deployment cell with no cross-cell market-data replication [Source: 03] [Committee].

Since this ADR was first drafted the platform has acquired durable state and an external witness, and both bear on where the boundary falls: a durable control-state store, one per service per deployment cell from shadow (ADR-018, D-058, O-115); a durable audit store with an **anchor directory owned by a different principal**, whose absence halts the platform (ADR-020, D-062, O-137); a **public trust set** read by every verifier, including installers and the CLI on operator machines (ADR-019, D-060); and tenant isolation as a server-side principal fact enforced by a predicate on every read and write (D-059, TC-TEN-001..004). A deployment cell is therefore no longer only a route boundary: it is a unit of durable state that must be **restored**, not merely re-routed [Committee].

Two clarifications of scope, because both have already caused confusion [Committee]:

- **Noisy-neighbour prevention was named in the original Context and this ADR does not deliver it.** Route isolation between planes is delivered by ADR-001 and tested by TC-NET-001..004. Tenant data isolation is delivered in software and tested by TC-TEN-001..004. **Resource isolation between planes and tenants is delivered by nothing today**: `infra/kubernetes` holds five namespaces and nine network policies and contains no ResourceQuota, LimitRange, PodDisruptionBudget or autoscaler [Open: O-160]. This ADR must not be cited as providing it.
- **"Cell" is used in this repository with a second, unrelated meaning.** A `JurisdictionCell` is a six-dimension compliance tuple (country × customer type × broker × venue × asset class × feature) whose enablement requires a dual key (NFR-GLO-01, D-050, `services/compliance/compliance_engine/eligibility.py`). It is **not** a deployment unit and nothing in this ADR grants, withholds or affects a market enablement. Throughout this ADR the unit defined here is written **deployment cell** in full (C-007-1).

## Decision

1. **Term.** The unit defined here is the **deployment cell** (written in full at first use in any document, and throughout any document that also discusses enablement). It is distinct from the `JurisdictionCell` of NFR-GLO-01 and D-050, which is a compliance enablement tuple and is never a deployment unit. No document may use the bare word "cell" for a deployment cell where a `JurisdictionCell` could be meant. **Provisioning a deployment cell enables no market, and enabling a jurisdiction cell provisions nothing** [Committee; D-050].

2. **Definition.** A deployment cell is defined by **the residency region it serves**. It contains the five plane namespaces and the plane network policies of ADR-001, and every durable store of the tenants it serves: the control-state store per service (ADR-018, O-115), the bitemporal market-data store and its archive (D-056), and the audit store (ADR-020). The venues a deployment cell's Execution plane may route to are an **attribute** of that cell, not its definition; one deployment cell may serve several venues, and a venue may be served from more than one deployment cell provided each cell holds only its own tenants' data. Residency, not venue proximity, is the partitioning key, because every durable store this platform has is bound to a residency region and none is bound to a venue [Committee; D-056, NFR-PRV-01, NFR-GLO-01].

3. **Venue placement is an open, measured question, not a decision of this ADR.** Whether the Execution plane serving a venue must be placed relative to that venue — for latency, connectivity, session, account-structure or certification reasons — is unevidenced in this repository: no broker, venue or provider document, quote or measurement exists here. It is decided at Gate C on measured baselines and on the broker certification record, never on a date and never on an assumption [Open: H-05, H-07, O-03, O-32] (C-007-5).

4. **The deployment cell is the failure domain, and the list of what may cross a deployment-cell boundary is closed, explicit and directional.** Exactly four items may cross, each with its direction and its property; nothing else may:

   | # | What crosses | Direction | Property it must have |
   |---|---|---|---|
   | (i) | **Audit replication** of the audit chain (ADR-020) | outbound from the deployment cell, to a destination outside it | append-only; never a read on the decision path; its lag is the recovery-point input of NFR-DR-01 [Open: O-18] |
   | (ii) | **Audit anchor publication** — a sealed `ChainHead` (length, head hash, time) and nothing else (ADR-020) | outbound from the deployment cell, to a principal outside it | see item 5; it is a distinct flow from (i) and must not be co-located with it |
   | (iii) | **Portfolio roll-up** | outbound from each contributing deployment cell into a read model | **read-only, derived and eventually consistent. It is never an input to a risk, eligibility or approval decision**, which remain pure functions of cell-local recorded inputs (NFR-DET-01). It is never on a recovery path. It carries a **residency question** — positions rolled from one region into a view served in another cross a residency boundary — which is the Privacy Lead's and counsel's to answer per jurisdiction, not this board's [Open: O-09, O-10, F-7] (C-007-6). Its carrier is undecided [Open: R-05] |
   | (iv) | **The public trust set** (ADR-019) | inbound to every deployment cell, and to every verifier outside every deployment cell | public verification material only; it contains no private key and no tenant, market or order data. It is **distributed as a reviewed, signed artefact to every verifier — including installers and the CLI on operator machines — and is therefore not a cell-to-cell dependency**. An absent trust set is an empty trust set and refuses everything (fail closed) |

   **Market data never crosses a deployment-cell boundary** (D-056). **No Control-plane or Execution-plane request, and no order, command, decision, approval, limit, lease or Kill Switch state, crosses a deployment-cell boundary in either direction.** **FX rates do not cross deployment-cell boundaries**: an `FxSnapshot` is ingested into each deployment cell and handed to the engine and the ledger as a recorded input, never fetched by them across a boundary (D-064; ADR-021 is proposed in `docs/SESSIONS/BUILD_E04_2026-09-08_fx_decision_input.md` and **no ADR-021 file exists in `docs/ADRs/` yet** [Open: O-171 proposed]); a missing, stale or unbudgeted rate halts with `RK-FX-MISSING` / `RK-FX-STALE` / `RK-FX-UNDEFINED` and is never treated as unlimited. Adding a fifth item to the list requires a new ADR and this board; it is never a deployment or a capacity decision, and no throughput, cost or availability argument may add one (CAPACITY_MODEL §0) (C-007-3).

5. **The witness of a deployment cell's audit trail lives outside that deployment cell.** The anchor directory of ADR-020 — the WORM bucket or replica written by a different principal — must be in a **different failure domain** from the deployment cell whose chain it witnesses, and must remain reachable by the standby that takes that cell over. Rationale, and it is the reason this item is in the Decision and not in a runbook: a missing or stale witness fails closed and carries the `killswitch_platform` auto-action (ADR-020, O-137); the documented recovery is restore-then-verify and disabling the check is never the fix (O-134, O-165). A witness co-located with the deployment cell it witnesses therefore converts the loss of one region into a **platform-wide halt that cannot honestly be lifted** — the exact blast radius this ADR exists to bound. The deployment identity of that principal remains [Open: O-54].

6. **Execution ownership is per account, not per deployment cell.** One active executor per account holds a leased lock with a monotonic fencing token (ADR-002, ADR-018); an account is served by exactly one deployment cell at any time. Active/standby is a property of the executor and of the deployment cell; it is not a licence for two deployment cells to hold live orders for one account.

7. **A tenant is not a deployment unit and may be present in more than one deployment cell.** Tenant isolation is a server-side principal fact enforced by a predicate on every read and write within a deployment cell (D-059). A tenant with accounts in several residency regions is served by several deployment cells; each cell is authoritative for the accounts, orders, decisions and market data it holds; **nothing is authoritative across deployment cells**, and the only cross-cell view of such a tenant is the derived roll-up of item 4(iii).

8. **What this ADR does not decide. This ADR decides no cell plan; no deployment cell is provisioned; a deployment cell has no capacity because there is no deployment cell** (C-007-2). How many deployment cells exist, in which regions, at what size, on which provider, with which storage and node classes, serving which venues, and when any of them is created, is **not decided here**: it is a human act requiring the operating entity's country (Q-11-1, O-66), the budget and provider decision (H-05) and the first-cell hypothesis (D-043). The inventory behind the second clause is `infra/`: five namespaces, nine network policies, one dev/sim compose file, and no other Kubernetes object kind and no IaC module [Open: H-05, A-6, B-15]. The third clause is literal: no CPU, memory, IOPS, storage, network, throughput, latency or cost figure is stated or implied by this ADR, and none may be derived from it. Nothing in this ADR authorises an environment or advances the environment ladder [Source: 00].

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why not |
|---|---|
| (a) **Single global deployment** | Rejected: unbounded blast radius, and it cannot satisfy residency per tenant region (NFR-PRV-01, NFR-GLO-01) or D-056's rule that market data does not leave its deployment cell |
| (b) **Deployment cell per tenant** | Rejected on **operational and control grounds**: a deployment unit per tenant multiplies the number of durable stores, audit chains, witnesses and DR drills linearly with tenants, while the isolation it would buy is already delivered and tested in software (D-059, TC-TEN-001..004). *The earlier version of this ADR rejected this alternative on cost. No cost figure exists in this repository and none is quoted; the cost comparison stays* [Open: H-05, O-87] |
| (c) **Deployment cell per venue cluster** (the earlier decision text) | Rejected as the **definition**, retained as an **attribute** (item 3). "Venue cluster" was never defined anywhere in this repository; it partitions on an axis to which no durable store of this platform is bound; and it forces a tenant with accounts in several countries either to place its data in a venue's region, breaking residency, or to place the Execution plane away from its venue, defeating the rule's own purpose. The latency and connectivity claim implicit in it is unevidenced [Open: H-05, H-07, O-03] |
| (d) **Deployment cell defined by residency region, venues as an attribute** (chosen) | Chosen: it is the only definition consistent with every durable store the platform has (ADR-018 control store, D-056 market-data store, ADR-020 audit store), with residency per tenant (NFR-PRV-01, NFR-GLO-01), and with a tenant that spans countries |
| (e) **Defer the whole ADR to Gate C with the provisioning questions** | Rejected: it leaves NFR-RES-01b, NFR-DR-01, CONTAINER_DIAGRAM, DR_PLAN and CAPACITY_MODEL resting on a proposal for a further gate (O-159) |

## Consequences

- **DR is per deployment cell, and a cell's recovery is now dominated by two unmeasured O(n) verifications.** A standby deployment cell must open a control store *and* an audit store, each verifying its own history, before trading reopens; DR_PLAN's order of restoration already puts audit verification ahead of the control and execution planes. RPO and RTO cannot be proposed until these are measured against a stated journal and chain length [Open: O-18, O-111, O-133, O-134, O-156, O-167].
- **The witness constraint of item 5 is a DR constraint, not only a security one.** The order of restoration must show the anchor directory as an input the standby reaches, not as something restored from inside the failed deployment cell. `docs/DR_PLAN.md` must be amended accordingly (C-007-4, owner Cloud Architect).
- **Cross-cell egress is a permanent cost term of this architecture, not an exception.** Audit replication and anchor publication cross a boundary by design. The storage cost model has no row for it and no measurement [Open: O-161, O-87, H-05]. No figure is stated here.
- **Residency is simplified for market data and unresolved for the roll-up.** D-056 removes market data from the cross-boundary question entirely; the roll-up puts it back for position data, per jurisdiction [Open: O-09, O-10, F-7].
- **Noisy-neighbour prevention is not delivered by this ADR** [Open: O-160]; see Context.
- **A tenant spanning regions has no authoritative global view**, by design. Any product surface that implies one is showing the derived roll-up of item 4(iii) and must be labelled as derived and eventually consistent [Open: O-122].
- **This ADR creates no infrastructure.** Every "per cell" statement in `docs/CAPACITY_MODEL.md` remains a **shape**, and its numbers arrive only after provisioning (H-05) and the Gate C baselines.
