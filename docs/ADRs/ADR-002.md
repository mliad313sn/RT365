## Context
Duplicate orders during failover are a named threat.

## Decision
One active executor per account holds a leased lock with a monotonic fencing token; every broker submission carries the token; stale tokens are rejected.

## Alternatives considered
(a) Leader election without fencing — rejected: split-brain risk. (b) Broker-side dedupe only — rejected: not all brokers support it. (c) Lease + fencing — chosen.

## Consequences
Requires lease store with strong consistency; adds failover test.
