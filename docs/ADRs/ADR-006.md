## Context
Mesh adds mTLS and policy but also complexity.

## Decision
Adopt mesh only for Control and Execution planes where mTLS and egress policy are mandatory; evaluate for Analytics at Gate B.

## Alternatives considered
(a) Mesh everywhere. (b) No mesh, library mTLS. (c) Selective — chosen.

## Consequences
