## Context
At-least-once delivery must yield exactly-once business effect.

## Decision
idempotency_key = hash(intent_id, account, policy_version); broker client_order_id derived from it; inbox dedupe on key.

## Alternatives considered
(a) Random UUID per attempt — rejected: retries create duplicates. (b) Broker-generated IDs — rejected: not available pre-submission. (c) Deterministic hash — chosen.

## Consequences
Intent re-evaluated under a new policy version becomes a new key by design.

## Controls and tests affected
FR-13; NFR-CON-01.
