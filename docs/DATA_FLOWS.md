## 0. How to read this document

- Every statement carries a tag: **[Source: NN]** (blueprint section as transmitted by the repository artefacts), **[Committee]** (the owner's or a council's reasoning from the artefacts), **[Open]** (unresolved, with the register item that settles it). No statement here is self-certified and nothing here approves anything [Committee].
- **Control rule (C-DF-1..C-DF-5, ARB §3.4):** a control appears in the *Controls* column only if the *Implemented by* column names a code path **and** the *Tests* column names a test id. Everything else is written as a **target** with its [Open] reference, so that no target can be read as evidence [Committee].
- **Number rule:** this document contains no throughput, latency, storage or cost figure. Capacity figures live in docs/CAPACITY_MODEL.md and are [Open] until measured [Committee].
- **Columns.** *Source → Sink* names the producing and consuming component; *Plane(s)* the trust planes crossed [Source: 03, 04]; *Carrier* how the data actually travels (in-process call, store file, bus, HTTP, file system); *Classification* is **proposed by the Data Architect and decided by the Security & Privacy Board** — every cell in that column is a proposal until the Board's classification review is recorded [Open: O-106, O-129]; *Implemented by* names modules and symbols (not line numbers, which drift) read at commit `ded8e17` on 2026-09-08 [Committee]; *Status* separates what dev/sim implements from what is a target.
- **Scope of the evidence.** Everything marked "implemented dev/sim" is evidenced only in the dev and sim environments; no market, broker capability, data entitlement, regulatory permission or production behaviour is asserted anywhere in this document [Source: 00] [Open: O-12, H-08, R-05, R-06].
- Residency and transfer per tenant are maintained in docs/PRIVACY_IMPACT.md [Source: 06]; §4 carries the pointer and the market-data residency rule decided in D-056.

## 2. Data at rest — the artefacts under `store_dir` and `anchor_dir` [Committee]

| Artefact | Flow | Written by | Contains | Classification (proposal) | Separation rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `control_state.sqlite` | DF-10, DF-11 | the platform process (control and execution tables in dev/sim) | orders and their command MACs, indexes, leases, outbox, inbox, activations — and the journal's second copy of all of it | Confidential | one store per service per cell from shadow; the Execution-plane store physically separate (D-058 (a), O-115) |
| `revocations.jsonl`, `nonces.jsonl` | DF-12 | the MCP-side runtime | revoked grants, consumed nonces | Internal | apart from Execution-plane state from shadow (D-058 (e), O-118) |
| `audit_state.sqlite` | DF-21 | the audit service | the audit chain and its seals | Regulated record | never the control store's file, transaction domain or restore unit; a different owner and backup schedule from shadow (ADR-020, O-136) |
| `audit_anchors.jsonl` under `anchor_dir` | DF-22 | a **different principal** (`audit-anchor-publisher`) | chain heads only | Internal | a store the audit process does not own; a WORM bucket or a replica in deployment [Open: O-54] |

Truncating the audit trail without detection therefore requires write access to two stores owned by two principals [Committee]. The control store's own integrity direction is to anchor its journal head into the audit chain rather than to introduce a keyed secret inside the Execution plane [Open: O-128, O-110].

## 3. Classification scheme used above [Committee] [Open: Security & Privacy Board review]

| Class | Meaning | Handling consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Public | verification material and published references; no confidentiality requirement | integrity and provenance only (DF-20) |
| Internal | operational state with no personal or licensed content | access control; integrity where it bears on a control |
| Licensed, non-personal | third-party data whose use is bound by a licence | entitlement check at the source, masking of unlicensed fields, redistribution limits [Open: O-12] |
| Confidential | account, order and position data and their identifiers | tenant predicate, masking or pseudonymisation before any Analytics boundary, encryption at rest [Open: F-7] |
| Personal | principal and actor data, sessions | residency per tenant, minimisation, redaction in telemetry [Source: 06] |
| Regulated record | audit and reporting records | append-only, retention schedule, legal hold, external witness [Open: O-09, O-54, O-135] |
| Secret | signing and credential material | never persisted by the platform, never reachable by AI/MCP, vault/KMS custody [Open: O-53] |

Every classification cell in §1 and §2 is a **proposal by the Data Architect**; the Security & Privacy Board decides the classification half of this document and the Privacy Lead reviews it [Committee] [Open: O-106, O-129].

## 4. Residency, transfer and knowledge time [Source: 06, 08] [Committee]

- The residency and transfer map per tenant is maintained in docs/PRIVACY_IMPACT.md [Source: 06].
- Market data is stored in the tenant's regional cell, knowledge time is enforced at the store, and there is no cross-cell market-data replication (D-056, ADR-007) — DF-01, DF-02 [Committee].
- Cross-cell traffic is limited to audit replication and the portfolio roll-up (ADR-007); the roll-up carrier is a target [Open: R-05].
- Residency enforcement at storage — encryption, region pinning, key location — does not exist yet [Open: F-7, H-05].
- Knowledge time is never later than decision time: a read whose `as_of` is after the knowledge time raises `LookAheadViolation` at the store rather than by convention (DF-01) [Committee].
