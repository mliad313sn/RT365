# goals/approvers and goals/counsel — approvers and counsellors created by the Product Owner (D-039)

Approvers chair the advisory councils and hold a delegated technical approval scope; the human Product Owner keeps every human decision, gate and risk acceptance. Counsellors advise any council and never approve. All are generated into `.claude/agents/` by `scripts/generate_agents.py` (kinds `approver` and `counsel`). Author != reviewer != approver is enforced by the write-scope guard: approvers and counsellors can write only session packets, RAID entries and (approvers) the reviewer column of the evidence index.
